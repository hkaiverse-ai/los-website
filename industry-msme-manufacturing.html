Industry focus

MSME Manufacturing

Practical, phased Lean and digitisation for small and medium manufacturers with limited time and budget.

Proposed page · awaiting company approval

This page describes common challenges and how Lean methods apply in this industry. It is not a statement of completed projects. It is hidden from search engines until the company confirms its experience in this sector.

Common operational challenges

  • Owner involved in every decision; few supervisors
  • Unorganised stores and shop floor
  • Delivery delays and rush orders
  • No regular production or quality data
  • Skills depend on a few experienced people

Relevant Lean tools

  • 5S
  • Basic VSM
  • Visual management
  • Standard work
  • Kaizen
  • Daily review meeting

Improvement opportunities

  • Quick visible wins in one area
  • Less searching and waiting
  • More reliable delivery
  • Skills spread across the team

Digitalisation & automation opportunities

  • Simple digital production and rejection logs
  • Daily report on a phone
  • Digital stores register
