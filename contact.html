What helps us understand your process

  • What you make and roughly how many people or lines are involved
  • The process or area that needs attention
  • What is going wrong today, and how you measure it (output, rejection, lead time, OEE…)
  • What a good result would look like, and by when
  • Any constraints: budget, shifts, systems, customer audits

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Operational Excellence?

Operational Excellence is a systematic approach to achieving superior performance by continuously improving processes, people, technology and management systems.

How is Leanova different from a traditional Lean consultancy?

Leanova integrates Lean, Six Sigma, Systems Thinking, Digital Transformation and AI rather than treating these as isolated disciplines.

Do you work with MSMEs?

Yes. Our solutions can be scaled according to the size, maturity and resources of an organization.

Do you provide training?

Yes. We provide customized corporate training and capability-development programs.

Can Leanova help with digital transformation?

Yes. We help organizations identify which processes should be digitized, automated or enhanced using AI.

Do you provide implementation support?

Yes. Depending on the engagement, Leanova can support organizations from diagnosis through implementation and sustainability.
