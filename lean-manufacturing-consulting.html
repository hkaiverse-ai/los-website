Service

Lean Manufacturing

A way of running production that removes waiting, excess stock, extra movement and rework, so work flows smoothly to the customer.

Discuss Your Process →All services →

The problem it solves

  • Orders take too long from start to dispatch
  • Material waits between machines or stations
  • People spend time searching, walking and waiting
  • Each shift works in a different way

What you can expect

  • Shorter lead time
  • Less work-in-progress and inventory
  • More output from existing resources
  • Clear, repeatable ways of working

Results depend on your starting point. We agree measures with you at the start and do not promise fixed percentages.

How Leanova helps

  1. Walk the floor

    Observe the work and map the flow from order to dispatch.

  2. Find the waste

    Mark waiting, excess stock, movement and rework.

  3. Fix one area

    Improve with your team in a pilot area first.

  4. Standardise and extend

    Write the new standard and roll it out.

Methods we use:

Value Stream Mapping5SStandard workKaizenVisual managementKanbanSMEDLine balancing

Common questions

Do we need to stop production?

No. Observation and most improvement work happen alongside normal production.

Where do we start?

Usually with one product family or production area, so you can see results before extending.
