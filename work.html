1 · Capabilities

What our technology team can build

Services describe the problems we solve. Capabilities describe the technology we use to solve them, in plain language.

AI agents

Software assistants that take a request, look up the information they are allowed to use, and complete a defined task, such as drafting a reply or preparing a report.

In business terms

  • Draft replies to customer emails for staff to approve
  • Prepare invoice drafts and spend summaries

Multi-agent systems

Several specialised agents working together on one job, each doing its part and passing the work on, much like a well-organised team.

In business terms

  • One agent writes content, another checks quality, a person approves
  • Engineering assistants that build and review each other's changes

Agentic AI architecture

Designing intelligent AI systems that can understand tasks, make decisions and coordinate multiple actions to complete business workflows, with limits, approvals and records built in.

In business terms

  • Spending caps and permission checks before every AI action
  • A full record of what each agent did and who approved it

Large language model (LLM) solutions

Using AI language models to read, summarise, answer questions about and write text from your own documents and data.

In business terms

  • Knowledge assistants that answer staff questions from your manuals
  • Summaries of long reports, contracts or meeting notes

Voice AI agents

AI that holds a natural spoken conversation, listens, responds and records what was said, with consent.

In business terms

  • First-round candidate interviews summarised for recruiters
  • Voice practice and coaching sessions for staff

Workflow automation

Connecting the tools you already use so routine steps (approvals, reminders, hand-offs and reports) happen on their own.

In business terms

  • Website enquiries go straight into your CRM
  • Weekly reports built and emailed automatically

Intelligent process automation

Automation combined with AI, so it can handle the less tidy steps, like reading an invoice PDF or sorting an incoming request, and route unusual cases to a person.

In business terms

  • Supplier and amount captured from invoice PDFs
  • Exceptions flagged for a person instead of guessed

AI-powered business solutions

Complete solutions that combine the capabilities above around one business goal, from forecasting to customer service.

In business terms

  • Demand or downtime forecasting from operational data
  • Quality prediction where reliable data exists
How we build AI

Every system we build follows the same rules

A clear job

Each agent has one defined task and nothing more.

Built-in limits

Permissions, consent and spending caps are checked automatically.

A person approves

Anything that matters waits for a human decision.

A full record

Every action is logged so it can be reviewed.
