Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Leanova Opex Solutions Private Limited ("Leanova", "we") handles personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in an enquiry: name, email, organisation, country, phone and your message.
  • Basic technical information needed to deliver the website securely.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare proposals you ask for.
  • To keep records of our communication with you.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (such as email hosting), and only as needed for that purpose, or where the law requires it.

Website chat and voice assistant

Chat. "Chat with us" answers common questions from the content of this website. Your messages are handled in your browser; they are not sent to us or stored. Please do not type sensitive personal information.

Voice. "Talk to us" connects you to an AI voice assistant run by our provider, ElevenLabs. When you start a call, your browser asks to use your microphone, and the microphone is used only while the call is on. The call audio and a transcript are processed and stored by ElevenLabs so the assistant can answer you, and Leanova may review them to follow up and to improve the assistant. ElevenLabs processes this data under its own privacy policy and may use other service providers. Please do not share sensitive personal information during a call. You can ask us to delete a call recording by writing to info@leanovaopexsolutions.com.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by writing to info@leanovaopexsolutions.com.
