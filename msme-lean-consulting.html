MSME Lean Consulting

Focus limited time and resources on the right improvements.

Leanova’s existing MSME focus combines Lean and operational improvement with practical capability building. Work starts with the business challenge and the resources available to sustain a change.

Discuss Your Process →All service areas →

Where it helps

Smaller manufacturing businesses can face inconsistent work practices, avoidable material movement, quality losses and limited visibility into delays. Improvement should fit the team’s capacity and day-to-day production commitments.

How we approach the work

Select a priority process, observe the work with its owner and agree a manageable first improvement. Methods may include workplace organization, visual controls, standard work, flow mapping and root-cause problem solving. Build practical skills as the process changes.

What to measure

Use a small set of measures the team can maintain, such as lead time, rejected or reworked output, output per labour hour and order completion. Compare results with a documented baseline before deciding what to do next.

Discussing fit and scope

Share your product or service, team responsibilities, production constraints and the problem you want to address. Budget, delivery format and availability are agreed during enquiry. This page does not claim government empanelment, scheme eligibility or subsidy support.

Guided by ASF™

The Adaptive Systems Framework connects the business context, constraints and improvement priorities. Choose methods to fit the problem, then measure outcomes and adapt.

Expected business benefits

  • A phased start that fits MSME budgets and time
  • Quick, visible improvements in one area first
  • Owner and supervisor capability built in-house
  • A foundation for later digitisation

Actual results depend on the starting point and scope. We agree baselines and measures with you; we do not promise fixed percentages in advance.

Industry applications

Small and medium manufacturers: job shops, fabrication units, component suppliers, food and consumer-goods units and family-run plants.

Tools and methodologies

  • Lean assessment
  • 5S
  • Basic VSM
  • Visual management
  • Simple daily review
  • Standard work
  • Kaizen

Who it is for

Owners and managers of micro, small and medium enterprises who need practical improvement within a limited budget and team.

Typical deliverables

  • A short diagnostic of the main losses
  • Phased plan of low-cost improvements you can start quickly
  • Simple standards, visual controls and record formats
  • Hands-on coaching for supervisors and key staff

Final deliverables are agreed for each engagement.

Frequently asked questions

Is this affordable for a small unit?

Engagements are scoped in phases, so you can start with a diagnostic and a focused pilot before committing to more.

Can you help with digitising records?

Yes. We can move paper registers and logs into simple digital formats when that will help you see and manage the losses.

Do you share results from other clients?

Only when a client has approved publication and the results are verified. Until then, we explain our approach and show labelled illustrative examples.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We reply by email to understand the process, the problem and your constraints, then suggest a sensible first step. That might be a short diagnostic visit or a call.
