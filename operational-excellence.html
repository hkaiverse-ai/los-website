Operational Excellence Consulting

Connect daily work with business performance.

Leanova connects process improvement, team capability and performance management so that improvement becomes part of daily work. Operational Excellence considers how decisions and activities across functions contribute to business results.

Discuss this business need →All service areas →

Where it helps

Teams may be busy while delivery remains unreliable, priorities compete and the same problems return. Disconnected departmental measures can also encourage local improvements that do not help the customer or the overall business.

How we approach the work

Use ASF™ to understand context, constraints and dependencies. Agree a few useful priorities and measures, assign ownership and improve the relevant process. Lean, quality methods, visual management and capability development are selected to suit the diagnosed need.

What to measure

Choose business and process measures together: delivery reliability, quality, throughput, lead time and closure of recurring problems. Review outcomes with the people doing the work and adapt priorities when evidence or business conditions change.

Where technology fits

Digital performance management can make information easier to use. Operational AI can be explored for forecasting or decision support when suitable data exists. Agent and LLM application development is a separate capability with readiness confirmed for each solution.

Guided by ASF™

The Adaptive Systems Framework connects the business context, constraints and improvement priorities. Choose methods to fit the problem, then measure outcomes and adapt.
