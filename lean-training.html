Corporate Lean Training

Help teams apply improvement methods to real work.

Leanova’s capability-development offering includes Lean, Six Sigma, manufacturing and quality topics. A corporate Lean programme can be scoped around the roles, current experience and operational challenges of your team.

Discuss Your Process →All service areas →

Who it is for

Operations leaders, supervisors, improvement practitioners and employees responsible for process performance need different levels of practice and detail. Start by clarifying what participants should be able to do in their work.

Topics to discuss

Lean fundamentals, recognizing waste, Value Stream Mapping, Kaizen, standardized work, visual management and improvement leadership are existing Leanova topics. Related manufacturing and quality topics can be discussed through the wider training catalogue.

How learning connects to work

Agree learning objectives, select suitable exercises and relate methods to an operational problem. Discuss how managers will support practice after training. Format, duration, delivery location and availability are confirmed during enquiry.

How to review progress

Review whether participants can describe the process, identify waste, use the selected method and explain an improvement measure. Business outcomes require application and follow-through. No accreditation, certification award, examination or guaranteed result is implied by this page.

Guided by ASF™

The Adaptive Systems Framework connects the business context, constraints and improvement priorities. Choose methods to fit the problem, then measure outcomes and adapt.

Expected business benefits

  • Teams who can run improvement without outside help
  • Shared language for problems and waste
  • Training linked to real projects on your floor
  • Future leaders identified and developed

Actual results depend on the starting point and scope. We agree baselines and measures with you; we do not promise fixed percentages in advance.

Industry applications

Operators, supervisors, engineers and managers in manufacturing plants and MSMEs; programmes are adapted to the audience and language needs.

Tools and methodologies

  • Lean fundamentals
  • 5S
  • Problem solving and A3
  • Kaizen facilitation
  • Standard work
  • Lean leadership

Who it is for

Organisations that want supervisors, engineers and managers to apply Lean and problem-solving in their own work.

Typical deliverables

  • Training plan matched to roles and current skill levels
  • Practical sessions using your own processes as examples
  • Applied exercises such as 5S, value stream mapping and root cause analysis
  • Follow-up coaching on real improvement projects

Final deliverables are agreed for each engagement.

Frequently asked questions

Is training delivered on site?

The format is agreed for each programme. Practical sessions work best close to the actual process.

Do participants receive a certificate?

Any certification or completion record is agreed in advance. No qualification is implied unless it has been agreed.

Do you share results from other clients?

Only when a client has approved publication and the results are verified. Until then, we explain our approach and show labelled illustrative examples.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We reply by email to understand the process, the problem and your constraints, then suggest a sensible first step. That might be a short diagnostic visit or a call.
