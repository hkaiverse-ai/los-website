How training helps the business

  • Improvements last because your own people run them
  • Problems are solved faster, closer to where they happen
  • Teams share one language for waste, quality and performance
  • Future leaders are developed from within

Lean training vs general employee development

General development builds broad skills such as communication or personal effectiveness.

Lean training is tied to how your plant works. People learn a method, apply it to a real problem in their own area and see the result.

Content, level, format, duration and language are agreed with you for each programme.
