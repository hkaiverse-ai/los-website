Training

Practical skills for better factory work.

Leanova’s training helps employees, supervisors, managers and leaders recognise problems and improve processes together.

Choose training around the team’s role.

Factory employees

5S, Lean basics and standard work

Learn to organise the workplace, spot waste and follow clearer work methods.

Supervisors & improvement teams

Kaizen, root-cause problem solving and continuous improvement

Learn to investigate recurring issues and test practical changes with the team.

Managers & technical teams

Lean Six Sigma, process measures and quality improvement

Learn to use data, understand variation and select improvement priorities.

Leadership teams

Lean leadership and daily performance management

Learn to support team ownership, review progress and sustain improvement.

Learning that connects to work

Use a real process challenge to make the topics useful. The goal is better problem solving and more consistent everyday work, rather than a list of technical terms.
