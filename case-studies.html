Productivity Improvement

Challenge
Low productivity and unbalanced production processes.
Approach
Value Stream Mapping, takt analysis, line balancing and standardized work.
Objective
Improve productivity and production flow.

OEE Improvement

Challenge
Low equipment utilization and frequent downtime.
Approach
OEE analysis, loss analysis, TPM and focused improvement.
Objective
Improve equipment effectiveness and production capacity.

HKAIVERSE Capability Studio

Practical AI capability, developed through scenarios and reflection.

HKAIVERSE is a related venture of Leanova OPEX Solutions, with its own platform identity. It is not presented as a client engagement or a Leanova consulting service.

The AI Capability Practice Studio describes scenario practice, explaining reasoning and decisions, and feedback on what a participant’s answers support. It helps identify a next area to practise; advisory feedback does not establish a qualification or guarantee job readiness.
