Our Work / Applications

Business problems. Practical applications.

Illustrative examples of how an improvement engagement could be approached.

These examples describe possible applications, not completed client engagements or verified results. Case studies will be shared when their context, implementation and outcomes are approved for publication.

Productivity Improvement

Challenge
Low productivity and unbalanced production processes.
Approach
Value Stream Mapping, takt analysis, line balancing and standardized work.
Objective
Improve productivity and production flow.

OEE Improvement

Challenge
Low equipment utilization and frequent downtime.
Approach
OEE analysis, loss analysis, TPM and focused improvement.
Objective
Improve equipment effectiveness and production capacity.

Quality Improvement

Challenge
High rejection and recurring defects.
Approach
Root Cause Analysis, SPC, Poka-Yoke and process improvement.
Objective
Reduce defects and improve process capability.
