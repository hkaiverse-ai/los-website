Factory Records Digitisation

Turn factory paperwork into data you can use.

Production logs, inspection sheets, shift handovers, maintenance registers and checklists hold valuable information, but on paper it is slow to compile and hard to analyse. Leanova helps you standardise these records, capture them digitally and use the data for daily decisions.

Discuss Your Process →All services →

What it means

Factory records digitisation means replacing paper registers and scattered spreadsheets with standard digital forms that operators and supervisors fill in at the point of work. The data then flows into shared reports instead of being re-typed at the end of the shift.

We do this after the process is clear and standard. Digitising a messy form only makes the mess faster, so we first agree what should be recorded, by whom and why.

Who needs it

Plants where supervisors spend hours compiling shift reports, where quality records are checked only when a customer complains, or where management cannot see yesterday’s output without a phone call. It suits MSMEs starting their digital journey as well as larger plants with many disconnected spreadsheets.

Common problems addressed

  • Paper registers that are incomplete, illegible or filled in after the event
  • Daily production and rejection figures re-typed into Excel
  • No single view of downtime, scrap or OEE
  • Audit and customer traceability requests that take days
  • Checklists signed but not acted upon
How Leanova approaches it

Our approach

  1. Inventory the records

    List every register, checklist and report, and who uses each one.

  2. Standardise

    Remove duplicate fields and agree one standard format per record.

  3. Digitise

    Build simple digital forms for tablet, phone or PC at the point of work.

  4. Automate

    Route approvals and alerts, and generate daily reports automatically.

  5. Review and extend

    Check adoption and data quality, then extend to more records.

Tools and methodologies

  • Record and data-flow mapping
  • Data dictionaries (agreed field definitions)
  • Standard work for data entry
  • Digital forms and checklists
  • Production and downtime logging
  • Quality inspection and rejection records
  • Maintenance and calibration logs
  • Workflow approvals and alerts
  • Dashboards and daily reports
  • Data-quality checks
  • AI-assisted document and data processing, where supported

Expected business benefits

  • Less time spent compiling and re-typing reports
  • Faster visibility of output, downtime and rejections
  • More complete and traceable quality records
  • A reliable data foundation for OEE, SPC and later AI use

Results depend on the starting point, scope and how consistently changes are sustained. We agree baselines and measures with you at the start; we do not promise a fixed percentage in advance.

Industry applications

Production, quality and maintenance records in discrete manufacturing (engineering, fabrication, auto components), batch records in process industries, and store and dispatch registers in MSMEs. Tool choice depends on your existing systems, budget and connectivity on the shop floor.

Typical deliverables

  • Record inventory and recommended standard formats
  • Digital forms for agreed pilot records
  • Automated daily production / quality report
  • User guide and short training for operators and supervisors

Final deliverables are agreed for each engagement.

From paper to intelligent records

What the journey can look like

  • Digital data collectionOperators enter output, downtime and checks on a tablet or phone at the machine.
  • Standardised digital formsOne agreed format per record, with required fields and valid ranges.
  • Workflow automationApprovals, escalations and reminders move automatically.
  • Production & quality recordsShift logs, inspection results and rejection reasons in one place.
  • Digital reportingDaily and weekly reports generated without re-typing.
  • AI-assisted processingWhere supported and validated: summarising records or extracting data from documents, always with human review.

These are examples of what is possible. The specific tools and features are agreed per engagement; AI-assisted functions are proposed only after checking feasibility on your records.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to buy an expensive ERP or MES?

Not necessarily. Many plants start with simple digital forms and reports, then decide later whether a larger system is justified. We recommend tools based on your needs, not a fixed product.

Will operators actually use it?

Adoption is the main risk, so we design forms with the people who fill them in, keep them short, and pilot one area before extending.

Can AI read our existing paper records?

AI-assisted extraction of information from documents is an area our technology team works on. Whether it suits your records depends on their format and quality; we check this on a sample before proposing it.

What about data security?

Where the data is stored, who can access it and how long it is kept are agreed with you as part of the scope.
