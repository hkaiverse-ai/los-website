Factory Records Digitisation

Turn manual paperwork into useful operational information.

Start with the process and the decisions your records need to support. Discuss how production logs, quality checks and daily reporting could move towards standardised digital forms and clearer workflows.

Discuss Your Process →

Who it is for

Manufacturing teams and MSMEs working with handwritten registers, duplicate spreadsheet entry, missing records or slow daily reports.

Problems to address

Unclear fields, inconsistent definitions, delayed information, transcription errors and repeated effort between production and quality teams.

Leanova’s approach

Map the record lifecycle, remove unnecessary entries and agree ownership. Define the minimum useful data, explore a suitable digital capture approach, pilot it with users, and review adoption and data quality.

Tools and methods

Process mapping, standardised work, data dictionaries, digital form design, workflow mapping and reporting requirements. Technology selection and integration depend on the plant’s systems and an agreed scope.

Practical applications

Illustrative opportunities include shift production logs, inspection checklists, downtime reasons and action follow-up. These are scoping examples, not claims of deployed software or client implementations.

Expected business benefits

Aim for more consistent records, less duplicate entry, clearer accountability and faster visibility. Agree a baseline and measures during discovery; no particular savings or result is guaranteed.

Automation follows a reliable process.

Workflow automation and AI-assisted information processing can be evaluated where data quality, access controls and human review support them. Availability and implementation scope must be confirmed for each solution.
