AI engineering

HK AI Works

In development

HK AI Works brings together the team’s engineering work on voice agents, agentic architecture, AI agents and LLM applications.

  • Voice agents. Spoken conversations that collect the purpose of a request and hand it to a person.
  • Agentic architecture. Designing how several AI components share a task, with clear permissions, review points and audit trails.
  • AI agents. Task-focused assistants that prepare a proposed action for a person to approve.
  • LLM applications. Reading, extracting from and summarising business documents, with citations back to the source.

This work is in development. We do not advertise finished products or client deployments here. If one of these areas matches a problem you have, we will tell you honestly what is ready, what would need building, and what it would take.
