Productivity Improvement Consulting

Find what limits useful output.

Leanova examines how labour, equipment, materials and information contribute to output. Productivity improvement begins by locating the constraint rather than assuming that faster individual machines or more activity will improve delivery.

Discuss this business need →All service areas →

Where it helps

Output may fall short despite overtime, equipment may be underused, or operators may wait for materials and instructions. These symptoms can come from different causes and need observation and reliable baseline information.

How we approach the work

Map the relevant process, observe cycle times and waiting, and examine the flow around the constraint. Depending on the issue, consider line balancing, material flow, workforce utilization, changeover reduction or equipment losses. Test a focused change before expanding it.

What to measure

Possible measures include throughput, output per labour hour, cycle time, capacity utilization and Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE). Define each measure consistently and check that improved output does not create quality losses or excess inventory.

Illustrative application

A line with uneven work allocation could compare task times, waiting and bottleneck output, then trial a different balance of work. Assess throughput and quality together. This is a practical example, not an assurance of savings or a client case study.
