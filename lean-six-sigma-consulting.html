Lean Six Sigma Consulting

Investigate recurring defects and process variation.

Lean Six Sigma combines a focus on customer value and flow with a structured investigation of variation. Leanova’s quality and process-improvement scope includes DMAIC, root-cause analysis, statistical process control and process capability.

Discuss Your Process →All service areas →

Where it helps

Recurring defects, rework, unstable cycle times and inconsistent output need more than repeated correction. A useful starting point is a clearly defined problem, a consistent measure and data that reflects how the process actually operates.

How we approach the work

Define the problem and the customer requirement, establish a measurement baseline, investigate likely causes and test changes. Use appropriate analysis for the available data, then agree controls and process ownership. The level of statistical work is scoped to the problem and data quality.

What to measure

Measures may include defect rate, first-pass yield, rework hours, cycle-time variation and process stability. Compare like-for-like conditions and distinguish a sustained change from normal variation. No percentage improvement is promised before diagnosis.

Illustrative application

For repeated dimensional defects, a team could check the measurement method, examine variation by machine or shift and test a suspected cause. A control plan would define how the team notices recurrence. This is an illustrative use case, not a claim of a completed engagement.

Expected business benefits

  • Lower variation and fewer chronic defects
  • Decisions based on data rather than opinion
  • Improvement projects with a clear business case
  • Internal team members able to lead DMAIC projects

Actual results depend on the starting point and scope. We agree baselines and measures with you; we do not promise fixed percentages in advance.

Industry applications

Processes with measurable variation: machining tolerances, filling and packing weights, coating and heat treatment, process industries and transactional processes such as order processing.

Tools and methodologies

  • DMAIC
  • Process mapping and SIPOC
  • Measurement system analysis
  • Hypothesis testing
  • Regression and DOE
  • SPC and control plans
  • FMEA

Who it is for

Quality and operations leaders facing recurring defects, rejections, process variation or customer complaints.

Typical deliverables

  • Problem definition with baseline defect or variation data
  • Root cause analysis using data (for example Pareto, SPC or process capability)
  • Countermeasures and error-proofing, verified with data
  • Control plan so the gains are kept

Final deliverables are agreed for each engagement.

Frequently asked questions

Is this certification training?

This page covers project-based consulting. For team training, see Corporate Lean Training. We do not issue certifications unless that has been specifically agreed.

Do we need clean data before starting?

No. Part of the work is deciding what to measure and setting up reliable data collection.

Do you share results from other clients?

Only when a client has approved publication and the results are verified. Until then, we explain our approach and show labelled illustrative examples.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We reply by email to understand the process, the problem and your constraints, then suggest a sensible first step. That might be a short diagnostic visit or a call.
