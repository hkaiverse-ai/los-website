Start with the problem

Busy all day, but still missing targets?

Late orders, repeated defects, excess inventory and long changeovers often come from the way work moves between people and machines. A Lean consultant helps you see that whole flow and focus on the constraints that matter.

  • Orders wait between steps while work piles up elsewhere.
  • Rework and scrap consume capacity and materials.
  • Machine downtime or uneven workloads limit output.
  • Improvements fade when daily routines are unclear.

We start with observation and your operating data, agree a baseline with your team, and choose a manageable area to improve first.

Lean consulting services

Choose the improvement your operation needs.

We select the tools around the problem. The work can begin with one process, one production line or a wider transformation programme.

Value stream mapping

Map the path from customer demand to delivery. Make queues, hand-offs and inventory visible, then define a future flow and an improvement plan with clear owners.

5S, Kaizen and standard work

Organise the workplace, make recurring problems visible and help teams test improvements. Document the agreed way of working so good practices survive changes of shift or staff.

Productivity and capacity

Review cycle times, line balance, layout and overall equipment effectiveness (OEE). Use SMED methods to examine changeovers and identify avoidable lost production time.

Kanban and pull systems

Connect replenishment to actual demand. Review work in progress, planning and material movement to reduce unnecessary inventory while protecting the flow to the customer.

Quality and problem solving

Investigate defects with root cause analysis and 8D. Apply mistake proofing (Poka-Yoke), FMEA or statistical process control where appropriate to prevent recurring failures.

Daily management and training

Build visual performance reviews, practical Lean training and clear follow-up routines. Help supervisors and teams take ownership of actions and sustain the improvements.

How we work

Understand, improve, then sustain.

The scope, measures and review rhythm are agreed with you before implementation.

01 · Understand

See the work as it happens

Discuss your delivery, cost and quality concerns. Review the process with the people doing the work and establish a starting point using available operating data.

02 · Improve

Test changes in a focused area

Prioritise actions by business value and effort. Work through a pilot with your team, measure what changes, and adjust before extending it to other areas.

03 · Sustain

Make improvement part of daily work

Set up standard work, visible measures and regular reviews. Assign owners so the team can keep identifying and solving problems after the initial project.

Common questions

Planning your first Lean improvement project.

Is Lean consulting suitable for a small business?

Yes. A focused engagement can start with a recurring problem such as delayed orders, excess stock or rework. You do not need to launch a company-wide programme to begin improving one process.

What should we bring to an initial discussion?

Describe the process, the main problem and the result you want to improve. Existing delivery, rejection, downtime or inventory records help us understand the starting point. We can also discuss gaps in the data.

How will we measure progress?

Choose measures tied to the problem, such as lead time, output, first-pass quality, changeover time or work in progress. Compare the baseline with results from the pilot and review whether the gains are sustained.
