Industry focus

Auto Components & Engineering

How Lean, quality and productivity methods apply to auto component suppliers, machine shops and fabrication units.

Proposed page · awaiting company approval

This page describes common challenges and how Lean methods apply in this industry. It is not a statement of completed projects. It is hidden from search engines until the company confirms its experience in this sector.

Common operational challenges

  • Frequent changeovers on shared machines
  • Customer PPM and delivery ratings under pressure
  • High WIP between machining, heat treatment and assembly
  • Rework from dimensional variation
  • Audit documentation kept on paper

Relevant Lean tools

  • VSM
  • SMED
  • OEE and TPM
  • Line balancing
  • Poka-Yoke
  • SPC
  • 8D
  • Kanban

Improvement opportunities

  • Shorter changeovers to support smaller batches
  • Lower in-process rejection and customer PPM
  • Stable flow between process stages
  • Higher OEE on bottleneck machines

Digitalisation & automation opportunities

  • Digital first-piece and in-process inspection records
  • Machine downtime logging and OEE reports
  • Traceability records for customer audits
