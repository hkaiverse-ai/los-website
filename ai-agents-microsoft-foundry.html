How we build it

One audited door between your agents and your systems.

Agents never reach a system directly. Every call passes through one gateway that checks what is allowed and writes down what happened.

  1. Your appWeb, chat or voice, with Microsoft Entra sign-in
  2. AI agentsAn orchestrator and specialists, one job each
  3. Tool gatewayAllow-list per agent, over MCP. Every call logged
  4. Rules engineYour business rule has the final say, not the model
  5. Your dataKept apart for each customer or business unit
Microsoft Foundry projectModels, tracing and evaluations. Called by the agents over a private network, with public access turned off.

Identity comes from sign-in

Microsoft Entra decides who is calling and for which customer. An agent cannot claim to be someone else.

Every call is logged

Each tool call leaves one audit record you can search and show to an auditor.

Rules beat the model

Where the AI's plan and your business rule disagree, the rule wins and the override is recorded.

Private by default

Models, data and agents sit on a private network. Nothing is exposed that does not need to be.

Spend has a ceiling

A cap for each customer and a monthly cap for the organisation, checked before every model call.

Changes ship switched off

Each new capability is turned on one at a time, after a named person approves.

Questions buyers ask

Straight answers.

Is your agent architecture running in production?

The products it serves are live, including the voice interviewers. The agent layer itself, with the tool gateway, the rules engine and the Foundry project, runs in our staging environment and stays switched off in production until each part is approved. We will walk you through the build and its test results.

Do you only work on Microsoft?

Microsoft Foundry and Azure are where we have built the most. The same controls apply on other platforms, and we will say so plainly if yours is outside our experience.

Who owns what you build for us?

You own the work built for you. We keep our own engine and tools, and you receive the right to use them in your solution. The contract states this before work starts.

What does the one-week review cost?

A fixed price, agreed in writing before we start. Ask and we will send it with the scope.

Where is your team?

Hyderabad, India. We work remotely by default and can be on site in India when the job needs it.
