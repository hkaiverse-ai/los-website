# MSME LEAN and ZED Schemes: Consultant Opportunities for Leanova

**Prepared:** 11 Nov 2026. **Status of Leanova:** not known to be empanelled under any scheme.
All sources were accessed on 11 Nov 2026. The source list is at the end, and the bracketed tags [S#] point to it.

> **Read this first: the scheme validity is unclear.** Both scheme guidelines (2022) say their financial support is "valid till March 31, 2026" [S3][S6]. Both portals still look active: Lean consultant trainings are scheduled for Nov–Dec 2026 [S2], and the ZED site shows "Last updated: 05 Nov 2026" [S5]. The FY 2026-27 Budget coverage refers to ZED and LEAN as components of the MSME Champions Scheme [S13]. **We found no official notice that extends the schemes or revises their terms.** Treat every figure below as subject to confirmation.

## 1. MSME Competitive (LEAN) Scheme (MCLS)

**Objective.** To improve the domestic and global competitiveness of MSMEs through Lean tools. The targets are lower rejection rates, less material movement and lower production cost, plus better use of space and resources [S3].

**MSME eligibility.** Any enterprise registered on the Udyam portal. Phase 1 covers manufacturing MSMEs, and Phase 2 is planned to add services [S3][S4]. SFURTI and MSE-CDP Common Facility Centres are also eligible [S4]. Each MSME must take a "Lean Pledge" first [S3].

**Levels and cost** (Guidelines 07-10-2022, para 4 and para 9) [S3]:

| Level | Mode | Duration | Max cost/unit | MSME pays | GoI pays |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | Self-paced e-learning plus online exam | up to 2 months | Free | – | – |
| Intermediate | Group of 4–10 MSMEs (GoE), on-site consultant | 6 months (+3 months grace) | ₹1,20,000 + tax | 10% (up to ₹12,000) | up to ₹1,08,000 |
| Advanced | GoE, on-site consultant, 24–26 man-days on site per unit | 12 months (+6 months grace) | ₹2,40,000 + tax | 10% (up to ₹24,000) | up to ₹2,16,000 |

- **Extra assistance.** GoI adds 5% for units in SFURTI clusters, women/SC/ST-owned units and units in the North-East Region. It adds another 5% for MSMEs that register through an OEM or Industry Association, and the OEM or Association receives ₹5,000 per MSME [S3].
- **Certificate validity.** 3 years. Intermediate and Advanced certificates have a surveillance audit at 18 months [S3].
- **Conflicting secondary figure.** Invest India (6 Apr 2023) lists ₹1.08 lakh and ₹2.16 lakh as level *costs* [S12]. The guidelines show these are the maximum *GoI share* [S3].

**How implementation works.** The Implementing Agencies (IAs) are the Quality Council of India (QCI) and the National Productivity Council (NPC) [S1][S4]. A Project Monitoring and Advisory Committee (PMAC) at DC-MSME approves GoEs and consultants [S3]. Stage 1 is the Diagnostic Study Report (DSR) and action plan, Stage 2 is implementation, and Stage 3 is a physical assessment by the IA together with the MSME-DFO [S3]. The MSME cannot choose its consultant. The IA allocates one through competitive bidding weighted 70% technical and 30% financial, although the MSME may submit a list of preferred organisations [S7].

**Role of Lean consultants** [S3]:
- prepare the DSR and action plan
- give remote and on-site handholding
- keep implementation records
- write case studies and GoE progress reports

A tri-partite agreement is signed between the GoE, the IA and the consultant. The same consultant cannot work on more than one GoE at a time.

**Consultant empanelment criteria.** These come from the "Consultant Empanelment Process" PDF on the Lean portal. **The PDF is undated, and the server file date is 10 Jul 2023, so it may be outdated** [S8].
- Only **consultant organisations** are empanelled, not individuals. An organisation empanelled by one IA is considered by all IAs.
- Eligible entity types include public, private and government companies, partnerships, societies, academic institutions and industry associations.
- **Documents:** registration certificate, PAN, GST registration, and the Aadhaar number of the CEO or Director.
- **Organisation experience:** 5–7 years for Intermediate and 7–10 years for Advanced. The organisation must have completed at least 2–3 Lean projects with demonstrated savings, backed by work orders or completion certificates. These projects must go beyond 5S alone.
- **Individual consultants:**
  - Qualification: engineering degree or diploma, MBA/PGDM, MCA/PGDCA, or M.Sc. Six Sigma or Lean Six Sigma Green Belt is preferred.
  - Experience: 5–7 years for Intermediate and 7–10 years for Advanced. Anyone with less than 5 years in Lean can only work as an Assistant Consultant.
  - Contract consultants must be backed by signed contracts.

**Portal and training.** The portal is https://lean.msme.gov.in. It has a "Register As Consultant" page with login, registration and a link to the empanelment PDF [S9]. On the access date the portal showed 211 registered consultants [S1]. It also lists 5-day "Consultant Training" sessions run by NPC, QCI and AIP. Upcoming sessions are in Pune (24–28 Nov and 1–5 Dec 2026), Chennai (30 Nov–4 Dec and 14–18 Dec 2026) and Patna (14–18 Dec 2026) [S2]. **The portal does not say who may attend these sessions or whether training is required for empanelment.**

**Not found.** We found no current QCI or NPC call (EoI) for Lean consultant organisations. QCI's MCLS EoIs found in search were for *Organising Partners* doing awareness programmes, a different role [S14]. Those QCI PDFs now return HTTP 404. NPC's MCLS page (npcindia.gov.in/NPC/MCLS) also returned 404.

## 2. MSME Sustainable (ZED) Certification Scheme

**Eligibility and process.** The scheme covers manufacturing MSMEs registered on Udyam, in NIC divisions 10–33 [S7b][S11]. Registration is free [S5]. The steps are [S5]:
1. Take the ZED Pledge.
2. Apply for Bronze, Silver or Gold.
3. Upload documents.
4. Undergo a desktop assessment (Bronze) or an on-site assessment.
5. Receive the certificate.
6. Claim incentives.

**Cost and subsidy** (Guidelines 2022) [S6]:
- **Certification cost:** Bronze ₹10,000, Silver ₹40,000, Gold ₹90,000, including taxes.
- **Subsidy on that cost:** 80% for micro, 60% for small and 50% for medium enterprises.
  - An extra 10% applies to SC/ST-owned units and to units in NER, Himalayan, LWE, island or aspirational districts.
  - An extra 5% applies to SFURTI and MSE-CDP units.
  - A ₹10,000 joining reward is given on taking the pledge.
- **Women-owned MSMEs:** 100% subsidy, under an addendum effective 11 Nov 2023 [S10].
- **Handholding:** available only after Bronze. Up to ₹5 lakh per MSME, of which up to ₹2 lakh is for handholding through consulting organisations and up to ₹3 lakh is for technology upgradation. The same 80/60/50% subsidy applies [S6][S11].

**Role of consultants and assessors.**
- **Consultants** do gap analysis and handholding. They must belong to a **QCI-empanelled consulting organisation** and must have passed ZED Consultant training [S7b].
- **Assessors** work for assessment agencies. The FAQs contradict each other on whether QCI or NABCB accredits those agencies [S7b].
- The MSME cannot choose its consultant or assessor [S7b].
- The FAQs say empanelment guidelines for consulting organisations "shall be uploaded soon". We could not find them on the site [S7b].

**How individuals qualify** ("ZED Trainings – Professional's Criteria", training.zed.org.in) [S11]:
- **Training:** a 5-day course with daily evaluation and a written exam on day 5. There is no fee, and no travel or daily allowance is paid. Passing does **not** guarantee any assignment.
- **Basic qualification:** a graduate degree or an engineering diploma.
- **ZED Consultant:** 10 years in manufacturing industry for graduates or 15 years for diploma holders, including at least 5 years in consulting.
- **ZED Assessor:** QMS/ISO Lead Auditor certification. Also 5 years in manufacturing for graduates or 8 years for diploma holders, including 3 years in a relevant role, plus at least 25 man-days of audits. Applicants nominated by Certification or Inspection Bodies get preference.
- **Technical knowledge:** at least one discipline in each of groups A, B and C. Examples include Lean, TQM, TPM, QMS, EHS and energy.
- One person cannot take both Assessor and Consultant training. Consultant applicants state whether they belong to a consulting organisation or work freelance.

## 3. Other empanelment routes (official sources only)

- **ni-msme, Hyderabad (central govt; a RAMP implementer in Telangana).** EoI published 11 Apr 2025 for empanelling service providers across India. Areas include "Lean" and "SME Consulting". Criteria were at least 5 years in business, 5 similar projects, average turnover of ₹1.5 crore over 5 years, and PAN/GST/TAN. There was a ₹10,000 processing fee and a ₹25,000 EMD, and empanelment lasts 2 years [S15]. **The deadline was 28 Apr 2025 and has passed.** Watch for the next call.
- **Andhra Pradesh (APMSMEDC, RAMP).** A June 2025 EoI to empanel agencies for ZED awareness and certification facilitation appears in search [S16]. **We could not read the PDF**, so its criteria and status are unverified.
- **Telangana:** we found no state RAMP or Lean consultant EoI.
- **SIDBI and Technology Centres:** not researched to an official source.

## 4. Action list for management

1. **Confirm that both schemes are live after 31 Mar 2026** and get the current guidelines. Ask DC-MSME, QCI-NDIE (zed.support@qcin.org [S7b]) and NPC.
2. **Lean readiness check against [S8].** Confirm Leanova has been in business for 5–7 years or more. Gather 2–3 completed Lean projects (beyond 5S) with savings data and signed work orders or completion certificates. Collect the company registration, PAN and GST certificates. Build the CVs, degrees and Lean/Six Sigma certificates of consultants with 5–10 years' experience.
3. **ZED readiness check against [S11].** Identify staff with 10+ years in manufacturing, including 5+ in consulting. Have them apply for ZED Consultant training on training.zed.org.in. **Do this only after management approval. We have not applied.**
4. **Monitor** QCI tenders (qcin.org), the NPC notices page, ni-msme tenders, and the TS and AP MSME portals for new EoIs.
5. **Timelines:** none are published for empanelment decisions. The only official durations are the MSME implementation periods (2, 6 and 12 months).

**Questions to put to the scheme office:**
- Is the 2023 Lean empanelment note [S8] still current?
- Is Lean consultant training required for empanelment, and who may attend?
- Is there an open window to apply as a Lean consultant organisation, and is it through QCI, NPC or the portal?
- Will there be a new ZED consultant-organisation EoI, and what are its criteria?
- Can one firm be empanelled for both MCLS and ZED?
- What are the rates paid to consultants?

## 5. What we must NOT claim

- Do **not** say Leanova is "empanelled", "approved", "registered", "accredited" or "authorised" under MCLS, ZED, QCI, NPC, ni-msme, RAMP or any state scheme until we hold written confirmation. Registering on a portal or completing training is **not** empanelment. ZED training explicitly guarantees no assignment [S11].
- Do **not** promise clients a subsidy, a level, a certificate or a timeline. Subsidies are decided by the Ministry and the IAs, and the scheme validity is unconfirmed after Mar 2026.
- Do **not** imply that MSMEs can pick Leanova under the schemes. The IAs allocate consultants [S7][S7b].
- Do **not** collect scheme fees. ZED states that no agency or consultant may charge registration fees and that payments go only through the portal [S5].
- Do **not** use government or QCI logos, or the "ZED" or "LEAN" scheme marks, in marketing.
