## Search terms to target (organic first)

Lean manufacturing consultant · Lean Six Sigma consultant · Operational excellence consultant · Productivity improvement consultant · MSME Lean consulting · Factory records digitisation · Lean consultant in Hyderabad *(only once service area is confirmed; the registered office is near Hyderabad but no service-area claim is made yet)*.

## Google Ads (later): starting recommendations

- Campaign: Search only, exact/phrase match on 10–20 high-intent terms above, Telangana/AP first (once confirmed), business-hours scheduling.
- Landing pages: the matching service page, not the homepage. Conversion = "Discuss Your Process" enquiry prepared + phone click + WhatsApp click.
- Negative keywords: jobs, course, certification, free PDF, training institute, salary.
- Small fixed monthly test budget, reviewed after 4–6 weeks on cost per qualified enquiry.
- Note: the website has no form backend, so enquiries are completed in the visitor's own email/WhatsApp. A real form endpoint (with spam protection and privacy review) would make ad conversions measurable and is recommended before spending on ads.
