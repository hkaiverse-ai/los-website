## Enquiry delivery

No contact submission endpoint exists in the public website. The inspected voice and ASF APIs are not enquiry-delivery endpoints. `assets/enquiry.js` prepares an encoded mailto message locally after required-field/email/whitespace validation. The visitor must review and send it using their own email application. A copyable text alternative, direct email and phone remain available. No data is posted, stored, sent automatically or reported as submitted. JavaScript-disabled visitors receive three direct email-topic links.

The latest requested categories take precedence: Improve Our Operations; Explore Digital & AI Solutions; Understand ASF™. Each AI offering carries a validated service identifier and preselects Digital & AI. Unknown query parameters are ignored and never rendered as HTML.
