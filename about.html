About Leanova

Better ways of working for IT businesses.

Leanova Opex Solutions Private Limited is a consulting and technology company focused on improving business processes, team effectiveness and digital workflows.

What “Opex” means

Opex stands for Operational Excellence: organising people, processes and daily decisions so an IT business delivers reliable service and keeps improving.

What Lean means in IT

Lean focuses on work that creates client value. It helps teams reduce waiting, unnecessary approvals, repeated data entry and avoidable rework.

Our approach

Improve the process before adding tools.

Understand everyday work

Focus on delivery delays, repeat service issues and fragmented information.

Connect the wider business

ASF™ helps identify related gaps and set useful improvement priorities.

Choose technology carefully

Use digitisation, automation and AI for a defined need, with clear ownership and human review.
