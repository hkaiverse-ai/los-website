About Leanova

Better operations start with better ways of working.

Leanova Opex Solutions Private Limited is a consulting and technology company that helps manufacturers improve how their factories work.

What “Opex” means

Opex stands for Operational Excellence: organising people, processes and daily decisions so the business delivers reliably and keeps improving.

Why Lean matters

Lean Manufacturing focuses on work that creates customer value. It helps teams reduce waiting, excess stock, unnecessary movement and rework.

What makes our approach different

Start with the problem, then choose the tools.

Practical process improvement

Focus on low output, recurring defects, delays and unreliable records.

A view of the whole business

ASF™ connects the issues so priorities support the wider business.

Technology that fits the work

Digitise an improved process before considering automation or AI. Build team skills alongside the change.
