About Leanova

Business improvement,
with people at its centre.

Leanova Opex Solutions Private Limited is an Indian Lean manufacturing and Operational Excellence consulting company, incorporated in August 2026 and registered in Telangana. We work on how a business operates: how work flows, how people solve problems and how decisions support performance.

Our focus is practical: productivity gaps, recurring quality issues, delays, disconnected workflows and limited visibility into performance. We connect process improvement and capability development with appropriate digital and AI solutions.

How we approach your business

Understand the problem before choosing the solution.

Start with the work

Understand objectives, observe the process and agree what improvement should look like. Lean and Operational Excellence address waste, variation, quality and flow; training helps teams sustain the changes.

Use ASF™ to set priorities

The Adaptive Systems Framework is our methodology for connecting business context, people, processes and technology. It helps identify constraints, prioritize improvements and adapt decisions as outcomes become clearer.

Choose technology with a purpose

Digital transformation connects information and workflows. AI-Enabled Operations explores forecasting, quality prediction and decision support where suitable data exists. The business need determines which tools to consider.

Our AI & Intelligent Automation work develops AI agents, large language model (LLM) applications and automated workflows for tasks such as finding business knowledge or preparing a response for review. These solutions are under development; readiness and scope are confirmed individually. They complement operational consulting rather than replace an understanding of the process.

Our philosophy

Build the ability to keep improving.

“Excellence is not a destination. It is a continuously improving system.”

We believe sustainable improvement happens when people, processes, technology and leadership work together. Our objective is to build capable organizations that can continuously improve themselves.

That means connecting process changes with team ownership, practical skills and measures of progress. ASF™ keeps the wider business in view as priorities and conditions change.
