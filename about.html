About Leanova

Who we are

Leanova Opex Solutions Private Limited is an IT services company. We help organisations use technology well: AI, automation, software and digital systems, built on clear and efficient business processes.

What “Opex” means for us

Opex stands for Operational Excellence: running processes so they deliver what the customer needs, reliably and without waste. We bring that discipline to every technology project, so the systems we build support work that already runs well.

Why the process comes first

Many IT projects fail because they automate an unclear or wasteful process. We map and simplify the process first. Then digitisation, automation and AI make a good process faster, instead of making a poor one faster.

Problems we help solve

  • Repetitive manual work and re-typing
  • Spreadsheets and paper used as business systems
  • Systems that do not share data
  • Reports that take days to prepare
  • Technology investments that do not deliver value

What makes Leanova different

  • Process before technology. We do not propose software until the process is clear.
  • Consulting and engineering in one team. The people who analyse the problem work with the people who build the solution.
  • A whole-organisation view through ASF™.
  • Honest about readiness. Every AI service shows whether it is available or in development.

The role of ASF™

ASF™, our Adaptive Systems Framework, is how we decide what to change first. It assesses an organisation across nine pillars, including technology and data, finds the constraints that hold results back and links each one to the right solution.
