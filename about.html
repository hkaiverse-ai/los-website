About Leanova

Who we are

Leanova Opex Solutions Private Limited is a Lean manufacturing and Operational Excellence consulting company. We help manufacturers make their factories work better, and then use technology to keep them working better.

What “Opex” means for us

Opex stands for Operational Excellence: running a business so that every process delivers what the customer needs, reliably and without waste, and keeps getting better. For Leanova it is not a one-off project. It is a way of working we help your team build.

Why Lean Manufacturing matters

In most factories, much of the time a product spends in the plant is waiting, moving or being reworked. Lean finds that waste and removes it, so the same people and machines deliver more, faster and with better quality.

Problems we help solve

  • Low output and long lead times
  • Recurring defects and customer complaints
  • High inventory but late deliveries
  • Paper records and no clear performance data
  • Improvements that do not last

Process first, then technology

We fix the process before we digitise it. Once a process is simple and standard, we move its records to digital forms, automate routine steps and, where the data supports it, use AI to help people decide. Technology then strengthens a good process instead of speeding up a bad one.

What makes Leanova different

  • Process before technologyNo software is proposed until the process is clear.
  • Consulting and engineering in one teamThe people who diagnose a problem work with the people who build digital tools.
  • A whole-business view through ASF™We look beyond one department before choosing what to change.
  • Honest about resultsWe agree measures at the start and publish only verified outcomes.

The role of ASF™

ASF™, our Adaptive Systems Framework, is how we decide where to start. It assesses a business across nine pillars, finds the few constraints that hold results back and links each one to the right improvement method.
