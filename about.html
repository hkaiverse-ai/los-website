Our background

From the shop floor to AI

Leanova began in operational excellence: helping manufacturing and engineering organisations remove waste, improve quality and raise productivity.

As our clients' processes improved, the next bottleneck was usually manual work: retyping, chasing approvals and building reports by hand. So we built a technology team to automate that work and, where it adds value, apply AI. Today one team delivers automation and AI, websites and software, and operations and people improvement.

Our approach

The ASF™ improvement cycle

Every engagement follows the same seven stages of our Adaptive Systems Framework, repeated as your organisation changes. ASF™ first finds where your observe–decide–execute–learn loop is stuck, then this cycle fixes it. How ASF™ works →

STAGE 1Understand

Objectives, context and current performance.

STAGE 2Diagnose

Observe the work and find the real causes.

STAGE 3Prioritise

Rank options by value and effort.

STAGE 4Design

Shape the change around real constraints.

STAGE 5Implement

Put it in place with clear owners.

STAGE 6Measure

Compare results with the starting point.

STAGE 7Adapt

Refine and choose the next improvement.
