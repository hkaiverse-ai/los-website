Quality & Process Excellence

Make processes reliable and defects rare.

Quality & Process Excellence combines what were two separate topics: making each process stable and capable, and stopping defects from reaching the customer. The focus is on the source of the problem, not on more inspection.

Discuss Your Process →All services →

What it means

Process excellence asks whether a process can produce the right result every time. Quality excellence asks how you detect, contain and permanently remove defects. They are two views of the same work, so we treat them as one service.

Where Lean Six Sigma is a full statistical improvement programme, this service is often more direct: solve the top recurring defects, standardise the fix and build a problem-solving routine your team can repeat.

Who needs it

Plants with high in-house rejection or rework, repeat customer complaints, frequent containment actions, or a quality system that exists on paper but not in daily practice.

Common problems addressed

  • The same defects return after corrective action
  • High rework and scrap costs
  • Customer complaints answered with containment only
  • Process variation between shifts, operators or machines
  • Inspection used as the main quality control
How Leanova approaches it

Our approach

  1. Prioritise

    Use rejection and complaint data to pick the defects that matter most.

  2. Contain and analyse

    Protect the customer, then find the root cause with your team.

  3. Fix at source

    Mistake-proof, standardise and control the process.

  4. Sustain

    Build a repeatable problem-solving routine and review cadence.

Tools and methodologies

  • Root Cause Analysis
  • 8D problem solving
  • Why-why and fishbone analysis
  • Process FMEA
  • Statistical Process Control (SPC)
  • Process capability
  • Poka-Yoke (mistake-proofing)
  • Control plans
  • Layered process audits
  • Quality management system support

Expected business benefits

  • Fewer recurring defects and repeat complaints
  • Lower rework and scrap
  • More consistent output between shifts and operators
  • A team that can solve the next problem without outside help

Results depend on the starting point, scope and how consistently changes are sustained. We agree baselines and measures with you at the start; we do not promise a fixed percentage in advance.

Industry applications

Machining and fabrication, assembly lines, auto components, plastics, food and process manufacturing: anywhere a defect has a measurable cost or a customer impact.

Typical deliverables

  • Defect Pareto and prioritised problem list
  • Root-cause and corrective-action records (e.g. 8D)
  • Updated control plans, standards and mistake-proofing
  • Problem-solving training for the team

Final deliverables are agreed for each engagement.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from Lean Six Sigma?

Lean Six Sigma is a broader, project-based programme using DMAIC and statistics. Quality & Process Excellence is usually more focused: solve the most costly recurring defects and build a routine to keep solving them.

Do you help with ISO 9001 or IATF certification?

We can strengthen the processes a quality system relies on. We do not issue certifications, and audit readiness is scoped separately.

Can you work on a single product line?

Yes. Starting with one product family or defect type is usually the best way to show results before extending.
