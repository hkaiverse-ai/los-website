The problem it solves

  • The same defects return after corrective action
  • High scrap and rework costs
  • Customer complaints answered with containment only
  • Quality depends on inspection

What you can expect

  • Fewer recurring defects
  • Lower rework and scrap
  • Fewer customer complaints
  • Consistent output between shifts

Common questions

How is this different from Lean Six Sigma?

This service focuses on solving your most costly defects quickly. Lean Six Sigma is a broader, data-heavy project programme.

Do you provide ISO certification?

No. We strengthen the processes a quality system relies on; certification is done by accredited bodies.
