Our strategic differentiator / ASF™

See the system.
Choose the next useful improvement.

ASF™ — the Adaptive Systems Framework — is Leanova’s structured approach to understanding a business, finding constraints and choosing where to improve first.

It connects people, processes and technology so a change in one area supports the wider business. Review measurable outcomes and adapt the next step.

Why Leanova

How we work differently.

  • Process before technologyWe fix the flow first, then digitise or automate. That way software doesn’t lock in waste.
  • A system view with ASF™We look at how strategy, people, process and data interact before choosing what to change.
  • Consulting and engineering togetherThe people who diagnose the problem also work with the people who build the tools.
  • Your team owns the resultTraining and coaching are part of the work, so improvements last after we leave.
  • Honest about readinessWe tell you plainly what is proven, what is in development, and what your data can actually support.
  • Measured outcomesWe agree baselines and measures at the start, and review them together.
