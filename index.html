Our framework

ASF™: the Adaptive Systems Framework

ASF™ is how Leanova looks at a whole business before deciding what to improve.

It assesses the business across nine pillars, from strategy and customers to operations, technology, people and adaptability, then finds the few constraints that hold results back and chooses the right method to fix them.

Explore ASF™ →Open ASF™ Analysis ↗ (opens in a new tab)

ASF Analysis is a separate application and needs an authorised access key.

  • Looks at the whole business, not one department
  • Based on evidence, not opinion
  • Picks the improvement that matters most first
  • Reviews results and adapts
