The Leanova approach

Improve the process first. Then add technology.

  1. Understand the current process

    Walk the floor, map the flow and agree baseline measures.

  2. Identify waste and problems

    Find waiting, rework, excess inventory and the real constraints.

  3. Improve with Lean

    Fix the flow with your team using proven Lean and Six Sigma methods.

  4. Digitise records and workflows

    Replace paper registers and spreadsheets with standard digital forms.

  5. Automate and add AI where it fits

    Automate routine steps; use AI only where data and process are ready.

  6. Measure and sustain

    Review results against the baseline and build routines that hold the gains.

Our differentiator · ASF™

The Adaptive Systems Framework.

ASF™ is Leanova’s framework for looking at a business as one connected system, so that an improvement in one area supports the whole enterprise.

Business purpose: it gives owners and plant leaders a structured, evidence-based way to see where the business stands, find the real constraints and decide what to improve first, then review the results and adapt.

  • Prioritise improvements by business value, not by the loudest problem
  • See how operations, people, technology and data affect each other
  • Base decisions on evidence and agreed measures
  • Keep adapting as conditions change, using Plan-Do-Check-Act
