TODAY / MANUAL PROCESS

Information takes time to find.

  1. Record production on paper.
  2. Retype the figures in a spreadsheet.
  3. Chase missing entries and prepare a report.
PROPOSED / DIGITAL WORKFLOW

Capture once. Review together.

  1. Enter agreed details in a digital form.
  2. Flag missing information for a person to check.
  3. Prepare a report for the responsible team to review.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things you may want to know.

How do we get started?

Share the process you want to improve or the digital solution you need. We’ll discuss the challenge and a suitable next step.

How much will the project cost?

Cost depends on the scope, complexity and support required. These are discussed and agreed before work starts.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

Not necessarily. We first review your current tools and processes, then assess what can be reused or connected. Any integration depends on access and technical suitability.

What happens after implementation?

Handover and any training or ongoing support are defined in the agreed project scope. Contact us to discuss the support your team needs.
