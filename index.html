Our strategic differentiator / ASF™

See the system.
Choose the next useful improvement.

ASF™ — the Adaptive Systems Framework — is Leanova’s structured approach to understanding a business, finding constraints and choosing where to improve first.

It connects people, processes and technology so a change in one area supports the wider business. Review measurable outcomes and adapt the next step.
