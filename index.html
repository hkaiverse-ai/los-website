Our framework

ASF™: the Adaptive Systems Framework

ASF™ is how Leanova looks at a whole organisation before deciding what to build or change.

It assesses nine pillars, from strategy and customers to technology, data and people, finds the few constraints holding results back, and links each one to the right solution.
