About this policy

This policy describes the Leanova website operated by Leanova Opex Solutions Private Limited. It covers browsing this website and preparing or sending an enquiry. The separate ASF Analysis application, HKAIVERSE platform and external services have their own information-handling arrangements.

Information you choose to provide

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, optional organization, chosen enquiry category and a description of your business challenge. These details are used in your browser to prepare a message. This website does not submit the form to a Leanova delivery backend or confirm that an email was sent.

Choosing Copy enquiry text places that message on your device’s clipboard when copying is permitted. If you open Gmail, Outlook or another email application, the recipient, subject and message are passed to that service to prepare a draft. Your chosen provider may require sign-in and handles the draft under its own privacy terms. Review the contents and sending account before clicking Send.

If you send an email or contact us directly, Leanova receives the information you include so it can respond to the enquiry and discuss possible work. Do not include passwords, API keys, confidential documents or sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry.

Browsing, hosting and external services

Your browser and the hosting service exchange technical information needed to deliver the website, such as your IP address and request information. Hosting-log handling depends on the hosting provider and deployment. Contact us for details about the current provider and retention arrangements.

The website code does not add advertising or analytics trackers, or store enquiry fields in local storage. It does not set its own tracking cookies. External destinations, including email providers, LinkedIn, Google Maps, ASF Analysis and HKAIVERSE, may process information under their own policies when you open them. The contact page uses an external map-search link rather than an automatically loaded map.

Enquiries and your choices

You can edit or abandon a prepared message before sending it, use your own email application or contact Leanova by phone. Closing a page does not remove content already copied to your clipboard or passed to your email provider; use that service’s controls to manage its drafts.

For questions about information you have sent to Leanova, or requests to access, correct or delete it, email contact@leanovaopexsolutions.com. Please describe the request without sending unnecessary identification documents. We will consider requests in light of applicable requirements and any information that must be retained.
