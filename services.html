Services

What Leanova does for manufacturers.

Each service solves a different kind of problem. Start with the one closest to yours, or ask us and we will suggest where to begin.

Lean Manufacturing

What it is
A way of running production that removes waiting, excess stock, extra movement and rework, so work flows smoothly to the customer.
Problem it solves
Long lead times, high work-in-progress, searching for materials and late deliveries.
How we help
We map your production flow, find the waste with your team, and fix it in one area before extending it.
Benefit
Shorter lead time, less inventory and more output from the same space and people.
Lean Manufacturing in detail

Lean Six Sigma

What it is
A data-based method (DMAIC: Define, Measure, Analyse, Improve, Control) for solving problems whose cause is not obvious.
Problem it solves
Defects or variation that keep coming back even after many fixes.
How we help
We run focused improvement projects with your team, using data to prove the cause before changing the process.
Benefit
Lower variation, fewer chronic defects and people who can run their own projects.
Lean Six Sigma in detail

Operational Excellence

What it is
A management system that keeps a plant improving every day: clear goals, daily reviews, quick problem solving and leaders who coach.
Problem it solves
Improvements that fade after a few months, and no regular rhythm for reviewing performance.
How we help
We set up simple daily routines, visual KPIs and escalation rules with your managers, and coach them until the routines run on their own.
Benefit
Gains that last, faster response to problems and better visibility of safety, quality, delivery and cost.
Operational Excellence in detail

Productivity Improvement

What it is
Getting more good output from the people and machines you already have, by finding and removing the losses that limit them.
Problem it solves
Output is below capacity, even though machines and people look busy.
How we help
We measure where time is lost (downtime, slow running, changeovers, imbalance) and remove the biggest losses first.
Benefit
Higher output per shift, better OEE and a clear picture of real capacity, often without new investment.
Productivity Improvement in detail

Quality & Process Excellence

What it is
Making each process reliable so defects are prevented at the source, not caught by more inspection.
Problem it solves
High rejection, rework and repeat customer complaints.
How we help
We use your rejection and complaint data to pick the costliest defects, find the root cause, mistake-proof the process and build a routine to keep solving problems.
Benefit
Fewer defects and complaints, lower rework cost and a team that can solve the next problem itself.
Quality & Process Excellence in detail

Factory Records Digitisation

What it is
Replacing paper registers, checklists and scattered spreadsheets with simple digital forms and automatic reports.
Problem it solves
Shift reports re-typed by hand, incomplete registers and no real-time view of output or rejections.
How we help
We first simplify and standardise each record, then move it to digital forms, automate approvals and reports, and add AI only where it is useful and tested.
Benefit
Less time on paperwork, faster visibility and reliable data for decisions.
Factory Records Digitisation in detail

MSME Lean Consulting

What it is
Lean improvement sized for small and medium manufacturers: practical, phased and affordable.
Problem it solves
Limited time, budget and supervisors, with the owner involved in every decision.
How we help
We start with one area and quick, visible wins (such as 5S and visual management), then build simple routines the owner and team can run.
Benefit
Visible results early, more reliable delivery and a team that improves without outside help.
MSME Lean Consulting in detail

Training

What it is
Practical Lean, quality and improvement training for operators, supervisors, managers and leaders.
Problem it solves
Improvement depends on a few people, and teams lack the skills to sustain changes.
How we help
Training linked to real problems in your plant, at the right level for each group.
Benefit
A team that can find and solve problems on its own.
