ASF™ · Adaptive Systems Framework

Find where the loop is stuck, then help people out of it.

ASF™ treats any person, team or organisation as a feedback loop. Every result depends on how fast that loop closes:

Observe→Decide→Execute→Feedback→Learn→Adapt

We measure six inputs that set the speed of the loop:

Observation latencyHow long until a problem is seen
Decision latencyHow long until someone decides
Execution latencyHow long until the decision is acted on
Feedback delayHow long until results come back
Learning velocityHow fast lessons change behaviour
DependencyHow much the loop waits on others
What ASF™ gives you

The gap, the reason, and the next step

ALS

The Adaptation Latency Score: one number for how fast the whole loop closes.

Primary bottleneck

The stage where the loop is stuck, so effort goes where it counts first.

CRT

For organisations, Capability Realization Time: how long until target capability is reached, using the nine-pillar architecture.

A learner

Is the student slow to notice mistakes, or slow to change after feedback? ASF shows which, and what to practise next.

A cricket batter

Reading the ball, choosing the shot, or adjusting after getting out: ASF finds the slowest link in the loop.

A pharma plant

From deviation detected to CAPA closed, ASF measures each delay and maps the gap to target capability.

So ASF™ finds the gap between target and current capability, shows why someone is stuck in the loop, and what to do next. In every engagement it decides what we improve or automate first, using our seven-stage cycle: understand, diagnose, prioritise, design, implement, measure, adapt.
