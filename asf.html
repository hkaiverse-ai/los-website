    ABC Manufacturing · Q3 margin review

    Summary

    Operating margin fell from about 9–12% to 1–4% in October to December because Supplier B cut steel coil deliveries. Stockouts, CNC-07 breakdowns and overtime followed. Margin recovered to 17% by March after a second supplier was approved.

    Key findings

    • SupportedSteel stock fell below safety level, with 11 stockout days in Q3. Inventory sheet
    • SupportedProduction fell to about 86% of plan while overtime spiked. Production, Workforce
    • SupportedCNC-07 accounts for the most repeat downtime incidents. Production Delays
    • InferenceThe recovery depends on keeping the second steel supplier; one supplier failing again would repeat Q3. Supplier Issues

    Contradictions found

    • ConflictNov-25 actual units: 40,750 in Monthly Summary vs 38,950 in Production.
    • ConflictJan-26 revenue: 515.9 in Monthly Summary vs 486.7 in Sales (6% higher).

    Next steps

    • Keep two approved steel suppliers and track on-time delivery monthly.
    • Run a root-cause review on CNC-07.
    • Correct the Monthly Summary before the next board pack.

    How it works

    From documents to a decision in four steps

    Answers are often spread across reports, spreadsheets and web pages. ASF™ reads them together and focuses the analysis on the decision you need to make.

    1. Sign in

    Use your Microsoft account. Leanova adds your work email to the pilot first.

    2. Add sources

    Upload company documents and add your website or other public pages.

    3. Describe the decision

    Give the business context, your objective and up to 20 questions.

    4. Analyze and download

    Read the report in the browser, then download it as PDF, PowerPoint, CSV or Markdown.

    What you can add

    Your own material, in the formats you already use

    Documents

    PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx, .xlsm, .xls), CSV and TSV, text, Markdown and JSON. Up to 25 MB each. Scanned PDFs need a text layer.

    Website pages

    Public pages that start with https://, such as your About, Products or Annual Report pages. Private and internal addresses are blocked.

    Up to 25 sources

    Combine documents and web pages in one analysis, so ASF™ can compare them with each other.

    What you get

    A report that shows its evidence

    The answer comes first and the detail follows. Every finding points to the source it came from and says how strongly the evidence supports it.

    SupportedProven by your documents
    InferenceOur interpretation of the evidence
    ConflictTwo sources disagree

    Choose an Executive, Detailed or Brief report style.

    Summary · the answer in a few lines
    Key findings · each with its source
    Contradictions · where your figures disagree
    Risks & gaps · what needs attention
    Next steps · suggested actions
    Downloads · PDF, PowerPoint, CSV, Markdown

    Questions

    About ASF™

    Do I need an API key?

    No. You sign in with your Microsoft account once Leanova has added you to the pilot.

    What can I analyse?

    Company documents (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, text, Markdown, JSON, up to 25 MB each) and public website pages, up to 25 sources in one analysis.

    How long does an analysis take?

    Typically 1–3 minutes, depending on how many sources you add.

    Which formats can I download?

    PDF, PowerPoint, CSV and Markdown.

    Is my material sent automatically?

    No. Nothing is uploaded or analysed until you press Analyze.

    Will ASF™ catch mistakes in my figures?

    ASF™ compares sources with each other and lists contradictions it finds, such as a summary sheet that doesn't match the detailed data.

    How much does it cost?

    The pilot is by invitation. Contact us for pricing that fits how your team will use ASF™.

    Is this the same as the ASF™ framework?

    No. The ASF™ (Adaptive Systems Framework) is our improvement method: it measures how fast a person, team or organisation closes its observe, decide, execute, feedback, learn and adapt loop, and finds the bottleneck. This engine is a software tool. Learn about the framework →

    How is ASF™ related to Leanova?

    ASF™ is developed by Leanova Opex Solutions Private Limited as a related venture, separate from our consulting services.
