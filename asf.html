ASF™: the Adaptive Systems Framework

ASF™ is Leanova’s framework for understanding a whole business, finding what holds its results back, and choosing the right improvement.

Explore ASF™ ↓Open ASF™ Analysis ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Why Leanova uses it

Many improvement projects fix one visible symptom while the real cause sits elsewhere: in planning, skills, data or decisions. ASF™ looks at the whole business first, so effort goes where it changes results.

What it helps analyse

  • Why delivery, quality or cost targets are missed
  • Where the real bottleneck or constraint is
  • Whether data and systems support good decisions
  • Whether people and leadership can sustain change
  • Which improvement to do first
How it works

From assessment to improvement, step by step.

  1. 01
    PLAN

    Understand the business

    Agree the objectives, scope and the results that matter.

    Example: on-time delivery has fallen from target and margins are under pressure.
  2. 02
    PLAN

    Assess the nine pillars

    Each area is assessed with evidence and rated on a maturity scale from 0 (absent) to 5 (adaptive).

    Example: interviews, records and shop-floor observation across operations, data and people.
  3. 03
    PLAN

    Find the gaps and constraints

    Identify the two or three weaknesses that are really holding results back.

    Example: high work-in-progress and frequent expediting point to a flow constraint in operations.
  4. 04
    PLAN

    Prioritise and choose the method

    The method follows the diagnosis, never the other way round.

    Example: long lead time and high WIP lead to value stream mapping and pull/Kanban.
  5. 05
    DO

    Implement

    Make the changes with clear owners and a clear sequence.

  6. 06
    CHECK · ACT

    Monitor and adapt

    Review results against the agreed measures, standardise what worked and adjust what did not, using the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle.

The nine pillars

Nine areas that together describe a business.

Each pillar is one part of the business. Looking at all nine shows how they affect each other: a quality problem may really start in planning, data or skills.

In practice

From symptom to the right method.

Strategy announced, but daily behaviour unchangedStrategy and leadership (Pillars 1 & 6)Policy deployment, objective cascade, leader standard work

ASF™, ASF Analysis and AI: what is the difference?

ASF™

The framework and methodology. Used by Leanova consultants in an engagement.

ASF Analysis

A separate software application that reads your documents and data and produces an evidence-linked report. Requires an authorised access key.

AI and LLMs

The technology that helps ASF Analysis read documents and draft findings. Every statement is checked against your evidence; people make the decisions.

ASF™ is not an ISO standard, a certification scheme, or a substitute for the requirements of any individual standard.

ASF Analysis · separate application

Apply business context to your evidence.

ASF™ is the method. ASF Analysis is the software application that applies it to your documents and data.

You describe the business context and objective. Then you add evidence: reports, spreadsheets or public web pages. The application reads the evidence, extracts signals, and produces a written synopsis. Every statement is checked against quoted evidence from your sources, and statements that can’t be supported are flagged.

What you provide
Business context (required), an objective, up to 20 questions, and up to 25 sources: PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, text, JSON or a public URL.
What you receive
An executive synopsis, key findings, important metrics, patterns, risks, opportunities, contradictions, unknowns and recommended actions, each with its evidence and confidence.
Report formats
On screen, plus downloadable reports.
Access
Authorised users only. The application needs an API key, and this website never asks for one.
