ASF™

Adaptive Systems Framework

Leanova’s framework for understanding how an IT business works, finding gaps and deciding what to improve first.

Why Leanova uses it

A delayed client release or recurring support issue may involve team capacity, handoffs, priorities and missing information at the same time. ASF™ helps examine those connections before choosing a solution.

What the nine pillars represent

Each pillar is a lens on the business. Together they cover the conditions needed to create value, manage performance and adapt to change.

From findings to improvement.

A plain-language summary of how Leanova uses findings; not a replacement for the official framework.

  1. 01

    Understand the situation

    Agree the business goal and review the available evidence.

  2. 02

    Find the gaps

    Use the pillars to see where information, process or capability needs attention.

  3. 03

    Agree and implement actions

    Choose priorities by impact, effort and dependencies. Assign owners and put the agreed changes into practice.

  4. 04

    Review and adapt

    Compare progress with the starting point and adjust the next action, using Plan–Do–Check–Act.

Example: client delivery is delayed

Check whether work waits for reviews, requirements are unclear or information is missing. Prioritise the cause, agree an action and review lead time and repeat work. This is an illustrative example, not a client result.
