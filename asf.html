ASF™: the Adaptive Systems Framework

ASF™ is Leanova’s framework for understanding a whole organisation, finding what holds its results back, and choosing the right change or solution.

Explore ASF™ ↓Open ASF™ Analysis ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Why Leanova uses it

Many technology projects fix a visible symptom while the real cause sits elsewhere: in the process, the data, the skills or the way decisions are made. ASF™ looks at the whole organisation first, so effort and budget go where they change results.

What it helps analyse

  • Why a system or technology investment is not delivering value
  • Where work gets stuck or repeated
  • Whether data supports good decisions
  • Whether people are ready for the change
  • What to do first
How it works

From assessment to improvement, step by step.

  1. 01
    PLAN

    Understand the organisation

    Agree the objectives, scope and the results that matter.

    Example: a new software platform has not delivered the expected benefit.
  2. 02
    PLAN

    Assess the nine pillars

    Each area is assessed with evidence and rated on a maturity scale from 0 (absent) to 5 (adaptive).

    Example: interviews, documents, system data and observation of how work is really done.
  3. 03
    PLAN

    Find the gaps and constraints

    Identify the two or three weaknesses that are really holding results back.

    Example: teams still re-enter data by hand because two systems were never integrated.
  4. 04
    PLAN

    Prioritise and choose the solution

    The method follows the diagnosis, never the other way round.

    Example: an architecture review and a benefits-realisation review before buying more software.
  5. 05
    DO

    Implement

    Make the changes with clear owners and a clear sequence.

  6. 06
    CHECK · ACT

    Monitor and adapt

    Review results against the agreed measures, standardise what worked and adjust what did not, using the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle.

The nine pillars

Nine areas that together describe an organisation.

Each pillar is one part of the organisation. Looking at all nine shows how they affect each other: a technology problem may really start in the process, the data or the skills.

In practice

From symptom to the right solution.

ASF™, ASF Analysis and AI: what is the difference?

ASF™

The framework and methodology, used by Leanova consultants.

ASF Analysis

A separate software application. You add business context and documents or data; it produces a report of findings, risks and recommended actions, each linked to its evidence. Requires an authorised access key.

AI and LLMs

The technology that helps ASF Analysis read documents and draft findings. Statements are checked against your evidence; people make the decisions.

ASF™ is not an ISO standard, a certification scheme, or a substitute for the requirements of any individual standard.
