Adaptive Systems Framework™

Understand the system.
Improve what matters.

ASF™ is Leanova’s structured approach to understanding business systems, identifying constraints and prioritizing improvements around measurable outcomes.

It connects business context, people, processes, strategy, technology and intelligence so improvement decisions reflect the whole organization.

Why connected thinking matters

Business problems rarely stop at a department boundary.

A local improvement can move a bottleneck somewhere else.

Late deliveries may involve demand changes, capacity constraints, quality problems and information delays at the same time. ASF™ helps examine their relationships before choosing an intervention.

Framework overview

From business context to adaptation.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Business objectives, context and current performance.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Observe work, examine evidence and identify gaps and dependencies.

  3. 03

    Prioritize

    Compare opportunities by business value and feasibility.

  4. 04

    Design

    Shape improvements around the real constraints.

  5. 05

    Implement

    Put agreed changes into practice with clear ownership.

  6. 06

    Measure

    Review outcomes against defined measures.

  7. 07

    Adapt

    Adjust the system as evidence and conditions change.

Consulting delivery

Turn understanding into practical improvement.

The existing consulting approach adds explicit discovery and sustainment activities to support delivery.

View our consulting delivery approach
Step 1 — Discover Understand the organization's strategic objectives, current performance and business challenges.
Step 2 — Diagnose Analyze processes, data, people, technology and organizational dependencies.
Step 3 — Prioritize Identify high-impact improvement opportunities based on business value and feasibility.
Step 4 — Design Develop practical improvement solutions.
Step 5 — Implement Support implementation through structured improvement projects.
Step 6 — Measure Track improvements using clearly defined KPIs.
Step 7 — Sustain Create management systems and organizational capabilities to sustain the gains.
Step 8 — Adapt Continuously improve the system as business conditions change.
