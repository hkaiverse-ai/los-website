Adaptive Systems Framework™ (ASF)

Connecting Strategy, Processes, People, Technology and Intelligence

Organizations operate as interconnected systems.

A problem in one area can create consequences across multiple functions.

The Adaptive Systems Framework™ provides a structured approach to identify:

ASF in Action

HK AIVERSE

Challenge

The organization required a structured method to measure student execution gaps dynamically, without penalizing learners for platform infrastructure latency. The core problem was accurately distinguishing true learner bottlenecks from systemic delays to ensure fair, data-driven journey adaptation.

Approach

Leanova integrated the Adaptive Systems Framework directly into the core architecture of HK AIverse, our own venture. We engineered a dual-instrument scoring engine that strictly isolates System ALS (platform latency) from Student ALS (learner execution). This engine continuously evaluates six critical dimensions — Observation, Decision, Execution, Feedback, Dependency and Learning Velocity — and translates them into actionable risk bands.

Result

The digital infrastructure now adapts to execution constraints across seven distinct journey surfaces, including AI tutor interactions, labs and navigation. Based on real-time ASF decisions, the system triggers targeted check-ins, optional scaffolding or standard guidance, bridging the gap between strategic goals and measurable execution.
