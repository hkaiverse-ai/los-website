ASF™ framework overview

From business context to adaptation.

The seven-stage overview connects business context, improvement decisions and adaptation.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Business objectives, context and current performance.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Observe work, examine evidence and identify gaps and dependencies.

  3. 03

    Prioritize

    Compare opportunities by business value and feasibility.

  4. 04

    Design

    Shape improvements around the real constraints.

  5. 05

    Implement

    Put agreed changes into practice with clear ownership.

  6. 06

    Measure

    Review outcomes against defined measures.

  7. 07

    Adapt

    Adjust the system as evidence and conditions change.

Consulting delivery

Turn understanding into practical improvement.

The framework overview guides improvement decisions. The consulting delivery approach organizes the engagement, including discovery and sustainment. Each serves a different purpose.

View the eight-step consulting delivery approach
Step 1 — Discover Understand the organization's strategic objectives, current performance and business challenges.
Step 2 — Diagnose Analyze processes, data, people, technology and organizational dependencies.
Step 3 — Prioritize Identify high-impact improvement opportunities based on business value and feasibility.
Step 4 — Design Develop practical improvement solutions.
Step 5 — Implement Support implementation through structured improvement projects.
Step 6 — Measure Track improvements using clearly defined KPIs.
Step 7 — Sustain Create management systems and organizational capabilities to sustain the gains.
Step 8 — Adapt Continuously improve the system as business conditions change.

ASF Analysis · separate application

Apply business context to your evidence.

ASF™ is the method. ASF Analysis is the software application that applies it to your documents and data.

You describe the business context and objective. Then you add evidence: reports, spreadsheets or public web pages. The application reads the evidence, extracts signals, and produces a written synopsis. Every statement is checked against quoted evidence from your sources, and statements that can’t be supported are flagged.

What you provide
Business context (required), an objective, up to 20 questions, and up to 25 sources: PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, text, JSON or a public URL.
What you receive
An executive synopsis, key findings, important metrics, patterns, risks, opportunities, contradictions, unknowns and recommended actions, each with its evidence and confidence.
Report formats
On screen, plus downloadable reports.
Access
Authorised users only. The application needs an API key, and this website never asks for one.
